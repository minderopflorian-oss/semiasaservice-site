What it is

Freight is moving toward electric trucks, and then toward trucks that drive themselves . Buying that equipment is a big step, especially while the technology is still arriving .

Semi as a Service is a different model . Carriers use the trucks, first electric and later autonomous, without buying them . You pay per mile, not per truck .

Who it is for

  • Carriers · operators who want to move goods electrically now, and be ready for autonomy later, without buying the trucks .
  • Shippers · companies that need reliable freight and want a cleaner, more efficient way to move it .
  • Fleet owners · people preparing to own and operate fleets, first robotaxis, then freight as the next step .
